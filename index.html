Adam Jackson-Smith

Actor & Writer

About

Adam originated the role of Basil Fawlty in John Cleese's 'Fawlty Towers: The Play' in the West End, and is currently writing the follow-up with John Cleese.

Adam trained at the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art and has been working professionally for over fifteen years. On stage, he has played leading roles in the West End and on tour. On screen, he has worked with the BBC, Amazon Studios, Netflix, Sky Atlantic and many more.

Acting CV

Adam is the co-creator of PRETTY PUNCHY, a fleet-footed development suite specialising in new writing projects and comedic adaptations for stage and screen.

Pretty Punchy

Adam is an experienced Corporate Role-player, working extensively with Red Leadership on crisis management and leadership communication deliveries. He is also an Acting coach and is currently a Core Tutor for Screen Acting at The Actor's Lounge.
